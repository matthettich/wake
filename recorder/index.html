Simple recorder · how to use it

Record your guitar, email it to yourself

Simple recorder records you playing on your phone and sends the recording to your email. Watch the short video, or follow the steps below.

Setting it up

Once
  1. 1

    Open the link in Chrome

    Tap Open Simple recorder above. A welcome note shows the first time. Press OK, LET'S START.

  2. 2

    Put it on your home screen

    Press INSTALL APP at the top of the app, then press Install. An orange icon with two tape reels, called Recorder, appears on your home screen.

  3. 3

    Open it from your home screen

    From now on, tap the Recorder icon to open it, like any other app.

Recording

  1. 4

    Press REC

    Press REC. The first time, the phone asks about the microphone: press Allow (it may say Allow on every visit or While using the app).

  2. 5

    Play, then press STOP

    The button turns orange and the clock shows how long you've been playing. When you're done, press ■ STOP. Your recording is saved straight away.

  3. 6

    Listen to it

    Press Listen. The picture of your recording turns orange as it plays; tap anywhere on it to jump to that spot. All your recordings are listed under My recordings, newest at the top.

Emailing it

  1. 7

    Press Email to myself, then Gmail

    Press the blue Email to myself button (or Email under any recording in the list). A menu of apps opens: choose Gmail.

  2. 8

    Type your address and press Send

    A new email opens with the recording already attached. Type your email address next to To, then press the Send arrow at the top.

If something goes wrong

Deleted one by mistake

Press UNDO at the bottom of the screen straight away. Missed it? Scroll to the bottom and tap Recently deleted, then Restore. Deleted recordings are kept there for 30 days.

"Too big to attach"

Long recordings can be more than Gmail takes (25 MB). When the app says so, choose Drive in the menu instead of Gmail. It saves to Google Drive, and you can share it from there.

It says it needs the microphone

You may have pressed Don't allow. In Chrome, open the ⋮ menu → Settings → Site settings → Microphone, and allow matthettich.github.io.

Using the USB microphone

Plug it into the phone before pressing REC. The app uses it on its own; the small line under REC shows which microphone is recording.

"New version ready · tap to update"

Tap it. The app gets better now and then; your recordings are kept.

Where recordings are kept

On your phone, in the app. Email the ones you want to keep, so there's a copy somewhere safe.
